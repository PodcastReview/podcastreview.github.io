01
英文材料｜JOEL SALATIN
TEDxMidAtlantic｜以下原文出自同一段连续讲话

Now, you’ve got to understand, when we raise chickens, we provide a habitat that allows the chicken to fully express its chickenness. The number one question is what is the essence of chicken? Because it is in answering that question, the chickenness of the chicken, that you actually get the best egg. Just like you get the best bacon from the essence of pig. You get your best T-bone, you get your best tomato from the essence of tomato.

See, we live in a culture today that doesn’t ask about the essence of pig; they simply ask how can we grow it fatter, faster, bigger, cheaper. That’s all that matters: faster, fatter, bigger, cheaper. And so we have this very mechanistic, in our Greco-Roman, Western, reductionist, linear, fragmented, compartmentalized, disconnected, democratized, individualized, parts-oriented thought process, we never think about the whole!

And so what happens is, as a culture, we basically view that pig or that chicken as just an inanimate pile of protoplasmic structure to be manipulated however cleverly hubris can imagine to manipulate it. And I would suggest that a culture that views its animals and plants from that type of manipulative, arrogant, disrespectful attitude, will also soon view its citizens the same way, and other cultures the same way.

So it is asking the chicken, “How can we fully allow you to express your chickenness?” that gives us not only the essence of chicken but therefore, the essence of egg.

本站译

我们养鸡时，会提供一种让鸡充分表现其鸡之为鸡的本性的生活环境。首要问题是：鸡的本质是什么？因为只有回答鸡之为鸡、鸡的本性这个问题，才能真正得到最好的鸡蛋。正如猪的本质带来最好的培根，牛的本质带来最好的丁骨牛排，番茄的本质带来最好的番茄。

我们今天的文化并不追问猪的本质，只问怎样让它长得更肥、更快、更大、更便宜。这就是唯一重要的事：更快、更肥、更大、更便宜。于是，在希腊—罗马传统下的西方文化里，我们形成了一套机械化、还原化、线性化、碎片化、区隔化、彼此割裂、民主化、个人化的部件导向思维；我们从不考虑整体。

结果是，我们把猪或鸡仅仅看成一堆没有生命的原生质结构，任由人类的自负想出多么聪明的办法去操纵它。我认为，一个以这种操纵、傲慢和不尊重的态度看待动植物的文化，很快也会以同样方式看待自己的公民以及其他文化。

所以，我们要问鸡：“怎样才能让你充分表现自己的鸡之为鸡的本性？”正是这个问题，让我们不仅得到鸡的本质，也得到鸡蛋的本质。

“Actually there are many different kinds of corn heaped together in this pile: George Naylor’s Pioneer Hi-Bred 34H31 mixed in with his neighbor Billy’s genetically modified 33P67; corn grown with atrazine mixed with corn grown with metolachlor. Number 2 corn is a lowest common denominator; all the designation tells you is that the moisture content of this corn is no more than 14 percent, and that fewer than 5 percent of the kernels exhibit insect damage. Other than that, this is the corn without qualities; quantity is really the only thing that counts. Such corn is not something to feel reverent or even sentimental about, and nobody in Iowa, save the slightly embarrassed agronomist, does.”

本站译

事实上，许多不同种类的玉米被混在同一堆里：乔治·奈勒种植的先锋杂交34H31，与邻居比利种植的转基因33P67混在一起；用莠去津种出的玉米，又与用异丙甲草胺种出的玉米混在一起。

二号玉米是一种最低公分母：这个等级只说明玉米含水量不超过14%，虫害颗粒少于5%。除此之外，这是没有自身品质的玉米；真正算数的只剩数量。这样的玉米不会使人产生敬意，甚至也不会使人产生感情。
